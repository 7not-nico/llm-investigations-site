Verification verdict

VERIFIED — MetaGlyph attribution and every operator number in the audit's fundamentals table. van Gassen, 2026 resolves; the ∩ 0–21% range, the 0% membership fidelity for OLMo-7B / Gemma-12B, and the 33.3% Llama-3B baseline all appear in the paper. The audit's "instruction-tuning degrades operator comprehension (mid-sized IT 7–12B: 0% vs base 3B: 33%)" is a fair reading — with one correction: the paper frames it as non-monotonic, not monotonic degradation. It reports a U-shape where frontier scale recovers fidelity. Read as "bigger is better" the claim is wrong; read as "small instruction-tuned models are worst" it holds.

REFUTED — the audit's model and layer attribution for negation circuits. meta-audit.md:190-196 states "Layer 10 :: Gemma-2-27B negation operator", an ablation collapsing downstream representations ~40%, and cites "Zhou et al., ICML 2026". The paper that matches the construction-vs-suppression description is arXiv:2605.03052, and it tests Mistral-7B and Llama-3.1-8B — not Gemma-2-27B. Neither the layer number nor the ~40% figure appears in its abstract. Either a different paper is meant, or the row conflates two sources. Until a Gemma paper is located, the Gemma/layer-10/40% row has no support.

VERIFIED with a scope caveat — the negation verdict. The audit's mitigation ("use X: disabled instead of do not X") is supported by Mason's register finding, not by the negation mechanism. MetaGlyph shows X: disabled-style declarative notation has its own measured fidelity spread (26%–91% on ∈), so the mitigation is a register change with a cost, not a free substitution.

CARRIED, NOT INDEPENDENT — the 87.5% priming row. This audit's fundamentals table cites Semantic Gravity Wells for "priming is the dominant negation failure mode" at the operator level (NOT / ! / ¬). The paper measures token bans. An operator-level verdict needs its own measurement; see the scope note in the gotcha-contraction raw folder, where the artifact itself is also checked.

VERIFIED — Rao et al. (arXiv:2604.13065). "100% of depth-7 errors have correct CoT but wrong declared answer" is exact: all 31 errors at Claude Sonnet 4 depth 7. This is the strongest single-paper row in the fundamentals table.

VERIFIED — Park & Lei (arXiv:2604.20564). The 41.1% fragility figure is real but lives in the body rather than the abstract, and it is reported as an asymmetry between high- and low-fragility connectives — so "one connective change derails 41% of correct chains" is a reasonable compression of a weaker statistic than it reads as. Fine as written.

VERIFIED — Jiang et al. (DOI 10.18653/v1/2026.acl-long.808, Immediate Inference: The Missing Foundation in Large Language Model Logical Reasoning, ACL 2026 Long). The abstract states it verbatim: models "lack robust operator grounding, oscillating between structural reasoning and surface pattern matching".

OVERSTATED — Tang (2026) as the operator-benefit verdict. The Cliff's δ < 0.01 result is real, but the paper studies compact constraint headers for code generation, not logical operators. The audit's row "Token reduction is the ONLY proven operator benefit" cites it as operator evidence; it is constraint-encoding evidence. The conclusion happens to be compatible — MetaGlyph measures the same non-effect on operators, at 62–81% reduction — but the citation does not support the claim it is attached to.

VERIFIED, VENUE CORRECTED — Li et al. on Chinese negation. The paper is A Study on the Reasoning Ability of LLMs Using Negation Principles in Chinese Sentences (Ran Li, Lingxiang Fan, Gan Ze, Zhaoyang et al.), available only as OpenReview e6NTBCmikA, and it is an AAAI 2026 Bridge workshop paper, not the main track. The intuitionistic/minimal-negation claim is in its abstract. A workshop preprint is thin support for a row the audit marks High confidence.

Open divergence

The audit's mechanism section ("early layers 0–40% parse operator syntax, late layers 60–100% process semantics") is a synthesis across sources, and no single verified paper states those percentages. Treat the band split as the audit's model of the evidence, not as a measured finding.
