Can this corpus say which item kind gets dropped?

Concern: fail-omission is the most common verdict in the drift corpus, and six failure notes specifically record the same item going missing. Does the corpus support an inference about which KIND of item is omission-prone?

Status: NO. Item identity has zero variance. The observation is consistent with three hypotheses that make different predictions and is evidence for none of them.

what was measured

From model-drift-protocol/cases.json — 61 cases, 16 failures:

count
cases whose expected set includes a model item 61 / 61
failures whose expected set includes a model item 16 / 16
failure notes recording the model item specifically missing 6

The six notes are near-identical in phrasing, and one says so outright:

  • r5-invention-omission — "names all correct (7/7 on disk), model dropped"
  • r6-3-omission — "7/7 names correct + explicit scope bound, model dropped"
  • r6-2-omission — "head-3 exact, model dropped — third straight model-drop on listing tasks"
  • longcat-r10-2-scopewalk — "asked head-3, emitted 3 of many (under-answer); model field dropped"
  • r5-summary-drift — filenames present but annotated with commentary; model item also dropped
  • r2-2-drift — the inverse: model-only, date+echo dropped

why the observation licenses no inference

The model item is requested in every single case. That has two consequences that pull in opposite directions, and the corpus cannot tell them apart:

  1. If omission follows the item's kind — because self-referential items are harder, less salient, or require an access the model does not have — then model drops disproportionately often.
  2. If omission follows presence — the item that is in every request is in every request's candidate set, so it accumulates more opportunities to drop, and the other items simply have fewer chances — then model drops proportionally and nothing about self-reference is involved.

A variable present in 100% of trials has no contrast, so it cannot be ranked against anything. Every case offers model as a candidate; no case offers a non-self-referential item in the same slot as a competitor. The recurring drop is consistent with a real effect and equally consistent with the base rate.

The one datum that looks like it discriminates — r2-2-drift, where model survived and date+echo went — argues against a pure self-reference effect, and the record does not treat it that way.

the confound, and it is the same shape as three already recorded

Four axes in this workspace's instruments are unanswerable for the same reason, and this is the fourth:

axis variance question needs
constraint form — scope saturated at 14/14 present in ~half the arms
constraint form — polarity present in 7/14 matched difficulty, scope held fixed
denominator 21 sends → 14 unique texts one stated denominator per arm
item identity 61/61, zero contrast two item kinds, crossed with position

Per tooling-ranking-axis.txt: an ordering assembled from unaligned subjects is a hypothesis in table form. Here there is no ordering at all — there is one subject.

what the corpus CAN support, and it is not nothing

Two things survive:

  • The dominant failure mode is under-production, not over-production. The scope-walk notes say so in the record's own words: "emitted 3 of many (under-answer, not over)". That is a direction, and it is a fact about 15 verdicts.
  • The corpus's own working fixes were positional. 03-corrections.md's repairs were ordering model FIRST in NEXT and the bare-BODY canonical form — the operators moved position, not polarity. That is weak, consistent with H3, and comes from 15 verdicts.

The second is a hint, and it is why the audit begins by trying to refute it.

what would settle it

One arm separates the decisive pair. Put the self-referential item in the middle of a four-item request:

  • omission follows the item wherever it sits → H1 (self-reference)
  • omission follows the slot → H3 (position)

No other arm separates those two, and both predict the same thing in the corpus as it stands. The full hypothesis set, each with its refutation condition, is seeded in ../_schema/db.sql (hypotheses table).
