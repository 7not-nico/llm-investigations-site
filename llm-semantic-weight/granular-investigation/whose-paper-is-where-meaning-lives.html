Whose paper is "Where meaning lives"?

Whose paper is "Where meaning lives"?

Concern: the audit's source table attributes "Where meaning lives: Layer-wise accessibility" to Skean et al. Does that paper have those authors?

Status: NO. The attribution is wrong. The audit merged one paper's title and finding with a different paper's authors.

the row

| *Where meaning lives: Layer-wise accessibility* | Findings ACL 2026 | Skean et al. |
  Lexical properties peak early; affective/semantic in middle. Final layer never
  optimal. Depth ordering shared across architectures. |

what each half resolves to

By title — arXiv:2601.03798, Where meaning lives: Layer-wise accessibility of psycholinguistic features in encoder and decoder language models, by Tikhomirova, Taisiia; Wulff, Dirk U., 2026-01-07. PDF in ../raw/.

By authors — arXiv:2502.02013, Layer by Layer: Uncovering Hidden Representations in Language Models, by Skean, Oscar; Arefin, Md Rifat; Zhao, Dan; Patel, Niket Naghi. PDF in ../raw/, deposited so the two can be compared.

Two distinct works. The audit's row takes the title and the finding from the first and the author list from the second.

the finding survives

Tikhomirova & Wulff do report what the audit's finding column says: lexical properties become accessible early, affective and semantic properties in the middle, and the final layer is rarely optimal for psycholinguistic features — with depth ordering shared across encoder and decoder architectures.

So this is an attribution fault, not a content fault. The row's claim is supported; its address is wrong.

why this one is worth a file

An attribution error is normally cheap: the reader follows the citation, finds a different paper, loses a minute. This one is not, for two reasons.

First, the misattributed paper (Skean et al.) is a real, plausible, on-topic paper about hidden representations in language models. A reader who checks will not find an error — they will find a paper that looks like it should be the source and is not.

Second, it is the third instance in these three audits of the same failure mode: a row borrowing another source's authority. The operator audit's negation row names a model family no source tested. The gotcha audit cites a Zenodo record that deposits no paper. Here the finding is intact and only the author is wrong — which is the subtlest form, because there is nothing visibly broken to find.

what to do

Change the author cell to Tikhomirova & Wulff, keep the finding, and keep the arXiv ID in the citation. The venue "Findings ACL 2026" is not confirmed from the abs page — treat it as unverified until a proceedings record is found.
